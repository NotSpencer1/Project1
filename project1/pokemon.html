Intro To My Hobby: Pokemon

I have been a fan of the Pokemon since I was a kid after my uncle got me some cards for my birthday, ive been obsessed ever since. To this day I still play the games and collect the cards and this is something ill probaly do for a long time.

My Favorite Pokémon: Sceptile

Out of over a thousand unique Pokemon, my absolute favorite is for sure Sceptile, also known asthe Forest Pokémon (#254). Sceptile was introduced in Generation III(my favorite generation)as the final evolution of the Hoenn Grass starter, Treecko. My Goat!

Why Sceptile is My Favorite

  • A. Design & Concept: Sceptile's design is an awesome reptilian like body with forest foliage features, with sharp leaves along its wrists that work as blades in battle.
  • B. Battle Performance: With an amazing Speed and Special Attack stats, Sceptile is useful as a fast paced offensive Pokemon, especially when using its signature move, Leaf Storm.
  • C. Mega Evolution: In Generation VI, Sceptile got a Mega Evolution that added the Dragon typing to its mono type, granting it higher stats and new resistances making it better defensively, as well as getting the Lightning Rod ability.
